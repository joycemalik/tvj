Campaign reference — Fvar per emission line, per year (all dated spectra)
Computed offline by run_variability.py: every spectrum in spectrum/ is fitted with this pipeline, dated from jd.xlsx, and the detected (≥3σ) fluxes of each line are grouped by calendar year. Fvar = √(σ²F − ΔF̄²) / F̄; error √(1/2N)·ΔF̄²/(F̄²Fvar) when σ²F/ΔF̄² < 10, else √(ΔF̄²/N)/F̄ (Vaughan et al. 2003; Sukanya et al. 2018). The fitted spectrum above is placed in this table in Graph 5.

3C 273 · Lyα Spectroscopic Pipeline

Automated spectroscopic fitting pipeline for 215 archival IUE SWP ultraviolet observations of the quasar 3C 273. Power-law continuum modeling, single-Gaussian emission-line decomposition over 5,600 candidate models, and quality-controlled flux, EW, SNR, FWHM measurements.
